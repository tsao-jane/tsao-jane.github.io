The loop every post goes through

AI helps in the middle; I own both ends

  1. Source researchFresh sources only. AI helps me scan; I pick what is worth a post.
  2. DraftAI drafts from my brief. I rewrite for the buyer and cut anything I cannot back up.
  3. VisualsA paired cover: the product in its real setting, made with AI image tools.
  4. ApprovalNothing is published without sign-off in the same conversation.
  5. SchedulingPosts are scheduled, with small scripts handling the repetitive parts.
  6. Read-back checkAfter it goes live, I re-read the post as a reader would and fix what is off.

What I did

  • Ran the showcase page since joining in December 2025. I did not launch it, so I only count the period I have run it.
  • Created and ran a persona account focused on industrial AI news, to test topics and reach.
  • Built the publishing loop above and ran it weekly for both accounts.
  • Made the covers and signed off every caption before it went out.

What I’m careful to say

  • The persona account is a brand persona I created and operate. I don’t name it here; its two best posts are shown above with the account name and avatar cropped.
  • All figures are organic. The showcase window is January 1 to October 28, 2026; the persona window is the 90 days before November 2, 2026.
  • I don’t claim ad results or paid reach: there were none in this work.
